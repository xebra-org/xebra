XDC, Sei, Unichain, World Chain or Codex, and it lists Stellar USDC but refused every quote from it:

Sources: `GET https://api.circle.com/v1/stablecoins` (`raw/circle_stablecoins_api.json`),
`GET https://1click.chaindefuser.com/v0/tokens` (`raw/oneclick_tokens.json`), and the Stellar-origin
quotes in `raw/oneclick_stellar_*.json`. HyperEVM (6.1 billion USDC) is left out because whether

All nine are CCTP chains. Xebra moves USDC from each of them to Base over CCTP, straight into the
deposit address 1Click issues, and NEAR Intents takes it from there
([guide](/docs/guides/near-intents)). The Stellar path is proven on mainnet: burn on Stellar at
23:24:27 UTC on 12 November, mint on Base at 23:24:38, USDC delivered on NEAR at 23:25:03, 36 seconds
end to end.

## Size: CCTP has no pool to run dry

CCTP burns on one chain and Circle mints on the other, so the size of a transfer is not limited by
anyone's liquidity. Pool and solver bridges are.

| | Largest size quoted | Refused at |
|---|---|---|
| Circle CCTP V2 | 10,000,000 per message, read on chain on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon and Avalanche; any number of messages | none |
| Across, Base to Arbitrum | 610,852 | 1,000,000 (`Max amount is 610851.84 USDC`) |
| Across, Arbitrum to Ethereum | 537,437 | |
| Relay, Base to Arbitrum | 1,000,000 | 5,000,000 (`Quote amount exceeds the maximum for this pair`) |
| NEAR Intents 1Click, Base to Arbitrum | 500,000 | 1,000,000 (`No liquidity available`) |

Xebra's own contracts cap each transfer while they are new: 100 USDC per transfer from Stellar
today, raised through a 48-hour timelock (see [Fees](/docs/concepts/fees)). The rail underneath has
no such limit.

## Cost: flat beats a percentage at size

Circle's fee for a Standard transfer is zero. Its Forwarding Service, which submits the mint on the
destination, charges a flat amount per transfer. Bridges that front liquidity charge a share of
the amount. For Base to Arbitrum:

| Amount | CCTP Standard + Circle delivery | Across | Relay | NEAR Intents 1Click | LI.FI | deBridge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 0.07 | 0.02 | 0.03 | 0.01 | 0.25 | 2.29 |
| 1,000 | 0.07 | 0.11 | 0.12 | 0.20 | 2.50 | 3.01 |
| 10,000 | 0.07 | 1.01 | 1.02 | 0.72 | 25.00 | 10.37 |
| 100,000 | 0.07 | 10.01 | 10.02 | 20.20 | 250.00 | 84.42 |

USD, total fees, from `quotes.csv`. Below a few hundred USDC the liquidity bridges are cheaper,
and much faster from rollups; above that, a flat CCTP route wins by more each time the amount grows.

## What integrating CCTP directly involves

CCTP is free to call. Using it across every chain means handling, among other things (each from
Circle's documentation):

- **31 domains**, each with its own domain id, and contract addresses that are shared on EVM chains
  except EDGE, where all four differ; Solana, Sui, Stellar, Aptos and Starknet each work differently.
- **Attestation polling** under a rate limit Circle documents as both 35 and 40 requests a second;
  going over it blocks every request for five minutes.
- **Attestations that expire** after 24 hours (counted in Ethereum blocks, even on rollups), then a
  re-attest and a second mint.
- **Fees that move**: Circle says to read them at least weekly and never hard-code them; a `maxFee`
  set too low quietly turns a Fast transfer into a Standard one.
- **Destination gas on every chain**, or Circle's Forwarding Service, which does not deliver to
  Stellar, Sui, Aptos, Starknet, Injective, Morph or Pharos.
- **Solana**: the mint goes to the recipient's token account, which may not exist yet; asking Circle
  to open it doubled the delivery fee in our sample (0.109 to 0.228).
- **Stellar**: 7 decimals against CCTP's 6, and inbound transfers must name Circle's `CctpForwarder`
  as both `mintRecipient` and `destinationCaller`. Circle warns twice that getting either wrong
  leaves the funds "permanently stuck and cannot be recovered".
- **Finality** from 5 seconds on Stellar to 15 to 19 minutes on OP Stack chains and Arbitrum, and 6
  to 32 hours on Linea.

With Xebra that is `GET /v1/quote` and `POST /v1/transfers`, or
[`@xebra/sdk`](/docs/sdk), on the routes [Xebra offers](/docs/concepts/routes).

## What these numbers do not show

- **Xebra is not cheaper than calling CCTP yourself.** Every Xebra route is CCTP plus Xebra's fee;
  what you save is the work above.
- **Xebra is not cheaper for small transfers.** At 10 to 100 USDC, Across, Relay and 1Click charged
  less on every EVM pair sampled, and Mayan's own CCTP route charged as little as 0.005.
- **Xebra is not faster from rollups.** It uses Standard finality: seconds from Stellar, Arc and Sui,
  15 to 19 minutes from Ethereum, Base and Arbitrum, where Across fills in seconds.
- **Stellar is not the only way into NEAR Intents for every asset.** It is the only path we found
  for Stellar USDC through 1Click. Allbridge Core supports Stellar but could not be reached when
  these numbers were taken.
- **Fees move.** These are one 25-minute window of quotes, not an average.
