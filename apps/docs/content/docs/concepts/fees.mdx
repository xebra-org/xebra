---
title: Fees
description: What a transfer costs, who is paid, and how the figures in a quote are made.
---

Every quote splits the cost into three parts, all in USDC and all taken out of the amount sent.
The recipient gets `amount` minus `fees.total`, and nothing else is charged in USDC.

| Field | Who is paid | When it is non-zero |
|---|---|---|
| `fees.xebra` | Xebra | Transfers through a Xebra fee contract (out of Stellar today) |
| `fees.circle` | Circle, for Fast Transfer | Fast transfers only |
| `fees.delivery` | Circle's Forwarding Service, for minting on the destination | Every route Circle forwards |
| `fees.total` | | The sum of the three |

The sender also pays the source chain's own network fee (XLM, ETH, SUI and so on) for the
transactions they sign. That is outside the quote.

<Callout title="Ask, don't compute">
Fees move with gas prices and with Circle's quotes. Call [`GET /v1/quote`](/docs/api/quotes/get-quote)
for the figure, and pass its `fees.total` as `maxTotalFee` when you build the transfer, so the API
refuses (`fee_above_limit`) if the price rose in between. A quote is good until its `expiresAt`, one
minute after it was made.
</Callout>

## Out of Stellar

Transfers out of Stellar go through Xebra's fee contract on Stellar. Its rule, as set on the
contract on 2026-11-13 (it was 0.10%, at least 0.30 USDC, from 2026-09-24):

- Xebra's base fee is **0.05% of the amount, at least 0.10 USDC**.
- Circle's delivery fee is paid **out of** that base fee, not added to it.
- If Circle's delivery fee is larger than the base fee, the sender pays Circle's fee and Xebra keeps
  nothing on that transfer.

In the quote's terms:

```text
total    = max(0.05% × amount, 0.10, delivery)
xebra    = total − delivery
received = amount − total
```

For example, with a delivery fee of 0.0538 USDC (Circle's quote for Base on 2026-11-13): 10 USDC
costs 0.10 in total, of which Xebra keeps 0.0462, and 9.90 arrives. 1,000 USDC would cost 0.50 in
total, but see the limits below. Where Circle's delivery fee is above 0.10 (Solana, about 0.13), the
sender pays only that and Xebra keeps nothing.

Stellar USDC has seven decimal places and CCTP has six. A transfer never burns a fraction of the
seventh place: anything below one six-decimal unit stays in the sender's wallet, and Xebra's share
is shown rounded up to six places.

### Limits

The contract takes **1 to 100 USDC per transfer** (as of 2026-11-12). Those limits stay low while
the contracts have had no external audit. The quote reports the live limits as `minimum` and
`maximum`; an amount outside them is refused with `amount_below_minimum` or `amount_above_maximum`.

The effective minimum can be higher than the contract's: Circle's delivery fee may be at most a
tenth of the amount (see below), so a route with a larger delivery fee needs a larger transfer. A new
Solana recipient, whose USDC account Circle opens, costs the most. `minimum` in the quote already
includes this, with a small margin for the fee moving.

## Out of EVM chains

Transfers out of an EVM chain, as the API builds them today, call Circle's `TokenMessengerV2`
directly. There is **no Xebra fee** (`fees.xebra` is `"0"`). The sender pays:

- `fees.delivery`: Circle's delivery fee for the destination.
- `fees.circle`: Circle's Fast Transfer fee, a small fraction of the amount, on Fast transfers only.

Xebra has a fee contract on Arc that applies the same rule as Stellar's. While it is switched on
for the site, the API does not build transfers out of EVM chains, and
[`POST /v1/transfers`](/docs/api/transfers/create-transfer) answers `route_unavailable` for them,
so that a transaction from the API never carries a different fee from the one the site shows.

## Out of Sui

Sui to Stellar has no fee from Xebra or from Circle as of 2026-11-12: every fee field is `"0"` and
the recipient gets the whole amount. The Stellar fee for the mint is paid by Xebra's relayer, or by
whoever claims it.

## Circle's delivery fee

Circle's Forwarding Service charges for minting on the destination and paying the gas there. It
differs by destination by about two orders of magnitude (cents on Arc, more on Solana, more again on
Ethereum-like chains) and moves with gas prices.

Because Circle keeps the whole fee the burn allows (see [Forwarding](/docs/concepts/forwarding)),
Xebra bounds it before anyone signs:

1. At most a **cap per destination chain**. On Stellar, the contract holds the cap for each
   destination, and a contract-wide ceiling of 2 USDC that no admin can raise.
2. At most **a tenth of the amount**. A transfer where fees would take more is refused with
   `amount_below_minimum`, and the error's `minimum` says how much would work.

## Ceilings no one can change

These are written into the contracts and cannot be changed by an admin:

- Xebra's percentage fee can never exceed 1%.
- Circle's delivery fee can never exceed 2 USDC per transfer through a Xebra contract.

Anything else that would raise a fee or loosen a limit waits 48 hours in public before it takes
effect. See [Security](/docs/concepts/security).
