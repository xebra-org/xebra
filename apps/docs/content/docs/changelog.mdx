## 0.3.0 (2026-11-13)

**API**:

- Transfers from Arc build again while Xebra's fee wrapper is on. `GET /v1/quote` from Arc now
  prices with the wrapper's live terms (its fee, minimum and maximum); before, it showed no Xebra fee
  and `POST /v1/transfers` refused. The approval and the burn go to the wrapper, and the burn is good
  for 30 minutes.
- EVM chains can send to each other, not only to and from Arc, wherever Xebra switches the pair on.
  `GET /v1/routes` lists them.

**SDK**, `@xebra/sdk` 0.3.0:

- `EnabledRoutes.evmMesh` and `parseEvmMesh`: EVM chains that all send to each other.
- [NEAR Intents](/docs/guides/near-intents): `pickHub` and `buildDeposit` work from Arc and the EVM
  chains as they did from Stellar. The guide shows an EVM sender.

Nothing in 0.2.0 changed.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-13)

**SDK**, `@xebra/sdk` 0.2.0:

- `@xebra/sdk/intents`, for [NEAR Intents](/docs/guides/near-intents): `isRouteAvailable(from, to)`
  answers from Xebra's live routes in NEAR Intents' chain names or Xebra's; `pickHub`, `usdcChainOf`,
  `buildDeposit` (send USDC straight to a 1Click deposit address) and `intentsAccountFor` (the NEAR
  Intents account a Stellar key already owns).

Nothing in 0.1.0 changed.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-12)

The first public release of the API and the SDK.

**API**, at `https://api.xebra.exchange/v1`:

- `GET /v1/routes`: every route Xebra sends on right now.
- `GET /v1/quote`: what a transfer costs and what the recipient gets.
- `POST /v1/transfers`: the unsigned transactions for a transfer, in signing order.
- `GET /v1/transfers/{chain}/{tx}`: where a transfer is, by its burn.
- `POST /v1/claims`: the unsigned mint for a delivery that did not complete on its own.
- `GET /v1/health`: liveness.

Errors are RFC 9457 problems with a stable `code`. The OpenAPI 3.1 document is published with the
SDK.

**SDK**, `@xebra/sdk` 0.1.0 on npm:

- `@xebra/sdk`: the typed API client, wire schemas and error codes.
- `@xebra/sdk/core`: chains, amounts, fee arithmetic, address checks and deep links.
- `@xebra/sdk/build`: build unsigned transactions against your own RPC.
- `@xebra/sdk/openapi`: the OpenAPI document.

## Planned

These are not released and have no dates yet:

- **API keys and webhooks**: keys with higher rate limits, signed webhooks for transfer status, and
  idempotency keys. See [Webhooks](/docs/guides/webhooks).
- **Integrator fees**: a fee you set on the transfers you send, paid out on chain. See
  [Integrator fees](/docs/guides/integrator-fees).
