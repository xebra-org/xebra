## Unreleased

**API**:

- Every route without a Xebra fee contract carries Xebra's fee: 0.01 USDC plus 0.01% of the
  amount, whether the transfer arrives by itself (Circle's Forwarding Service, or Xebra's relayer
  into Stellar) or the recipient claims it. `fees.xebra` shows it; `amount` is what leaves the wallet
  and the burn is the rest. See [Fees](/docs/concepts/fees).
- `POST /v1/transfers` returns a `fee` step after the burn from EVM chains (a USDC transfer) and
  from Aptos (a new `aptos_call` transaction). From Solana and Sui the fee is in the burn's own
  transaction.
- Aptos to Stellar needs at least about 1.0102 USDC (1 USDC burned, what the relayer completes).
- `invalid_request` and `invalid_amount` from checking the input carry `issues`, each field that
  failed as `{ path, message }`, beside the `detail` sentence. `openapi.json` lists it, and in
  `@xebra/sdk` `ProblemSchema` has it as optional and `XebraApiError` carries it on `extra.issues`.
- Sui to Stellar transfers built through `/v1` are now finished by Xebra's relayer, as the web app's
  are. When `GET /v1/transfers/sui/{tx}` sees an attested burn that has not been minted, it hands it
  to the relayer and answers `delivering` (with `deliveryTx` once the mint is sent) instead of
  `needs_claim`. `needs_claim` now means the relayer refused (its `reason` says why) or is switched
  off, and `POST /v1/claims` still finishes it. No new call, field or state.

**SDK**, `@xebra/sdk`:

- `planEvmFee` takes `xebraFee`; `FeePlan` gains `burn`. `XEBRA_FLAT_FEE`, `flatFeeFor`,
  `flatFeeKind`, `CLAIM_ONLY_DOMAINS`; `buildEvmFeeStep`, `buildAptosFeeStep`.
- `TransferStep.id` can be `"fee"`; `UnsignedTx` can be `aptos_call`.
- `createRebalancer().estimate`: `better` is `"unknown"` when the direct swap could not be priced
  (1Click unreachable, timed out, 429 or 5xx), not `"rebalance"`; `direct` carries the failure's
  `code` next to `reason`. An error that is not 1Click's is thrown instead of becoming `reason`.
- `isXebraApiError(e)` (additive): a type guard for `XebraApiError` that also works when two copies
  of the SDK are installed, where `instanceof` does not. It checks a brand,
  `Symbol.for("xebra.error")`, and the error's `name`.
- `XebraError` (additive): shared base error class exported from `@xebra/sdk` with `code`,
  `retryable`, and `cause`. `XebraApiError` now extends `XebraError` (keeping `instanceof
  XebraApiError` compatibility), preserves `cause` when wrapping errors, and marks `network_error`,
  `timeout`, `rate_limited`, `upstream_unavailable`, and `internal_error` as `retryable`.
- `OneClickError` (`@xebra/sdk/intents`, the market-maker rebalancer) extends `XebraError`. Its
  `code` is `missing_api_key`, `no_deposit_address`, `http_error`, `network_error`, `timeout` or
  `invalid_response` (an answer without the fields the rebalancer reads); `retryable` is true for
  network errors, timeouts, 429 and 5xx; `body` keeps what 1Click sent. `status` is unchanged.
- `CLIENT_ERROR_CODES`, `ClientErrorCode` and `XebraErrorCode` (additive): the codes the client
  throws on its own, and every code an `XebraApiError` can carry. `XebraApiErrorCode` is kept as
  the same type. `err.extra` types `minimum`, `maximum`, `have`, `need`, `total`, `limit` and
  `needs` as strings.

- `quote` and `createTransfer` check their input with `QuoteRequestSchema` and
  `TransferRequestSchema` before calling the API. Input it would refuse throws `XebraApiError` with
  `status` 0 and code `invalid_amount` or `invalid_request`, and the problems on `extra.issues`,
  without a request.

- `RebalanceError` (`@xebra/sdk/intents`): `submitWithdrawal` and `prepareDeposit` throw it, with
  `stage` (`withdraw`, `resolve`, `deposit_quote`, `build`), `code`, `retryable` and `cause`, and a
  message that says where the market maker's USDC is now. `prepareDeposit` is safe to call again.
  A route Xebra stopped running is `route_unavailable`; fees over `maxTotalFee` are `fees_above_cap`.
- `buildSuiMint` / `buildSuiMintStep`: mint a transfer bound for Sui (Circle does not deliver there)
  from any Sui wallet; `readSuiBoundMessage` reads its recipient and amount; a transfer already
  minted throws `AlreadyMintedError`. `checkSuiAddress`.
- `stellarDestinations({ sui: true })` adds Sui once the wrapper commits domain 8; `Destination`
  gains `claimOnly`.

**App**:

- Stellar to Sui, behind `NEXT_PUBLIC_STELLAR_TO_SUI=1` and the wrapper's domain 8. The recipient
  finishes it at `/claim`, which now mints any transfer bound for Sui.

## 0.4.0 (2026-11-15)

**SDK**, `@xebra/sdk` 0.4.0, `@xebra/sdk/intents`:

- `createRebalancer`: a market maker moves its own USDC inside NEAR Intents from one chain's asset
  ID to another's over CCTP (withdraw to its wallet, burn, 1Click deposit back to its account).
  `estimate` compares the cost with the same move as one swap inside NEAR Intents.
- `usdcChainOf` recognises Aptos USDC, and skips asset IDs 1Click marks `USDC(DEPRECATED)` (the
  HOT IDs that Omni IDs replaced on Avalanche, Polygon and BNB Chain on 2026-11-09).

The API did not change. Nothing in 0.3.0 changed.

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-14)

**API**:

- Solana, Sui and Aptos are source chains to every EVM chain Xebra lists and to Arc; Sui and Aptos
  also send to Solana, and all three to Stellar, where Xebra's relayer completes the mint. `GET
  /v1/routes` lists them; `POST /v1/transfers` from Aptos returns a step of kind `aptos`.

- Transfers from Arc build again while Xebra's fee wrapper is on. `GET /v1/quote` from Arc now
  prices with the wrapper's live terms (its fee, minimum and maximum); before, it showed no Xebra fee
  and `POST /v1/transfers` refused. The approval and the burn go to the wrapper, and the burn is good
  for 30 minutes.
- EVM chains can send to each other, not only to and from Arc, wherever Xebra switches the pair on.
  `GET /v1/routes` lists them.

**SDK**, `@xebra/sdk` 0.3.0:

- `EnabledRoutes.evmMesh` and `parseEvmMesh`: EVM chains that all send to each other.
- [NEAR Intents](/docs/guides/near-intents): `pickHub` and `buildDeposit` work from Arc and the EVM
  chains as they did from Stellar. The guide shows an EVM sender.
- `ChainId` includes `"aptos"`, and `TransferStep` a new `aptos` kind: Circle's precompiled CCTP V2
  burn script and its arguments. From `@xebra/sdk/build`, `aptosBurnPayload` turns it into what an
  Aptos wallet signs, and `aptosBurnArgs`, `buildAptosBurnStep` and `normaliseAptosAddress` build one.
- Sui: `buildForwardedBurn` and `buildSuiForwardStep` burn with Circle's forwarding, so Sui reaches
  every EVM chain and Solana without a claim on the other side.
- `stellarBurnRecipient("G...")`: the mint recipient and hook for a burn into Stellar from any chain.

Nothing in 0.2.0 changed.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-12)

The first public release of the API and the SDK.

**API**, at `https://api.xebra.exchange/v1`:

- `GET /v1/routes`: every route Xebra sends on right now.
- `GET /v1/quote`: what a transfer costs and what the recipient gets.
- `POST /v1/transfers`: the unsigned transactions for a transfer, in signing order.
- `GET /v1/transfers/{chain}/{tx}`: where a transfer is, by its burn.
- `POST /v1/claims`: the unsigned mint for a delivery that did not complete on its own.
- `GET /v1/health`: liveness.

Errors are RFC 9457 problems with a stable `code`. The OpenAPI 3.1 document is published with the
SDK.

**SDK**, `@xebra/sdk` 0.1.0 on npm:

- `@xebra/sdk`: the typed API client, wire schemas and error codes.
- `@xebra/sdk/core`: chains, amounts, fee arithmetic, address checks and deep links.
- `@xebra/sdk/build`: build unsigned transactions against your own RPC.
- `@xebra/sdk/openapi`: the OpenAPI document.
