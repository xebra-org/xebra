<Callout title="Coming" type="info">
  Integrator fees are not available yet. Today every quote's `fees` has three parts: `xebra`, `circle`
  and `delivery`. See [Fees](/docs/concepts/fees).
</Callout>

## What is planned

A wallet or app that sends transfers through Xebra will be able to add its own fee on top:

- **Set by you, capped on chain.** You choose your fee in basis points, up to a ceiling the contracts
  enforce.
- **Part of what the user signs.** The integrator and its fee are in the transaction itself, and the
  most the user agrees to pay covers every fee. A quote will always show your fee, so the user sees the
  full price before signing.
- **Paid on chain.** Your fees accrue to your address inside Xebra's contracts, and you withdraw them
  when you choose. Nothing is pushed to you during a user's transfer.
- **Disclosed in every quote**, alongside Xebra's and Circle's fees.

This needs new versions of Xebra's contracts on Stellar and EVM chains, which will be audited before
they carry money. This page and the [changelog](/docs/changelog) will be updated when it ships.
