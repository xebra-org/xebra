---
title: My transfer is stuck. Did I lose my money?
description: What to check when a USDC transfer is taking longer than expected, and how to get help.
---

Usually, no. A transfer moves in stages: your wallet sends USDC, Circle checks the transfer, then
USDC is minted to the recipient on the other chain. Most transfers take a few minutes overall.

Once Circle attests the burn, the USDC cannot be lost. Circle has signed proof of the transfer, and
the mint can still be completed by anyone. The recipient and amount cannot be changed.

## What happens, and how long does it take?

| Stage | What is happening | Usual timing |
|---|---|---|
| Sent from your wallet | The source chain records your transfer. | Usually seconds to a few minutes, depending on the chain. |
| Waiting for Circle | Circle is checking the transfer and preparing its signed proof. | Usually a few minutes after the source chain confirms it. |
| Sending to the other chain | Circle is minting USDC for the recipient. | Usually shortly after Circle's check; sometimes a few more minutes. |
| Arrived | USDC has been minted on the destination chain. | The transfer is complete. |
| Needs a claim | Circle checked the transfer, but automatic delivery did not finish. | You can complete it yourself; see [Needs a claim](#needs-a-claim). |

These are typical times, not a guarantee. A busy network or a delay at Circle can make a transfer
take longer. For a detailed status guide, see [Track a transfer](/docs/guides/track-a-transfer).

## My wallet showed an error

A wallet can show an error even if it already sent the transaction. Before trying again:

1. Open your wallet's activity and find the transfer. If it shows a transaction hash, open it in the
   source chain's explorer (often linked from the activity).
2. Check whether the explorer says the transaction succeeded, is still pending, or failed.
3. If it succeeded, the transfer went out. Save its hash and [check its status](/docs/guides/track-a-transfer).
   **Do not send it again**: that would start a second transfer.
4. If it is pending, wait for the explorer to update. Do not send again while you are unsure.
5. If it failed or there is no transaction in your wallet activity, it may not have been sent. If
   you are unsure, contact us before trying again.

Never share your wallet's recovery phrase or private keys with anyone.

## It says “Waiting for Circle”

This means Circle has not finished checking the transfer yet. It is normal for this to take a few
minutes after the source chain confirms it. Keep the transaction hash and check again later.

If the source chain shows that your transaction succeeded but it has been more than 30 minutes,
contact us with the transaction hash and the chains you were sending between. A delay does not mean
your USDC is lost.

## Needs a claim

This means Circle checked the transfer, but its automatic delivery did not complete. The USDC is
still meant for the recipient named when you sent it. Open [the claim page](https://xebra.exchange/claim),
connect a wallet on the destination chain, and follow the instructions. The wallet that submits the
claim pays that chain's transaction fee.

For more detail, see [Claim a stuck transfer](/docs/guides/claim-a-stuck-transfer).

## Where to get help

If you are unsure what happened, or your confirmed transfer has been waiting more than 30 minutes,
email [partners@xebra.exchange](mailto:partners@xebra.exchange) or message
[@xebra_exchange](https://x.com/xebra_exchange) on X. Include the transaction hash, the source and
destination chains, and what your wallet or transfer status shows. Never send your recovery phrase
or private keys.
